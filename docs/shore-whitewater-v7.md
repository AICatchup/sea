# Shore whitewater candidate v7

Candidate is explicit opt-in: `new ShoreSpray(renderer, ground, {whitewater:true})`. Default remains off. Root owns renderer integration and visual acceptance. Optional public `shoreSpray.whitewater.material` is available to the renderer's material prepare/compile lifecycle. Add it to that lifecycle before enabling the candidate; no renderer file is changed here.

The helper uses the existing spray 24×24, 6 m grid, 0.2 s request interval, 2304-byte RGBA readback. It requests no second framebuffer or GPU readback. Current breaker energy in B is recomputed from FFT finite-difference Jacobian, positive crest height, wet depth and shelter; it is **not** historical FFT foam alpha. The sample seam carries height, current compression-derived energy, depth, shelter, ground and coast gradient. The existing RGBA cache has already folded shelter into energy, so its adapter sets shelter=1 (do not interpret that as a separately measured shelter field). Cache age is checked against simulation time externally, at most 0.5 s. Missing/stale samples cull fragments rather than invent a replacement surface.

Birth requires energy >0.12, depth 0.2–3.8 m, shelter >=0.18, a finite nonzero bathymetric uphill gradient and ground at least 0.08 m below sampled water. Existing deterministic xorshift produces seed-dependent alongshore birth jitter, lengths, widths and lifetimes. Credits are reset when the sample footprint changes. Maximum 24 whitewater births/update and a fixed pool of 1024 fragments; occupied slots are never overwritten. Spray is unchanged.

Foam drifts onshore at an initial 0.45–1.5 m/s and slows analytically with 0.24/s drag; seeded alongshore motion is at most 0.25 m/s. Length and width spread with age. Lifetime is 2.2 + 3*energy + 1.8*seed seconds (up to 7 s). Coverage rises in 0.18 s then fades with age. Wet-strand depth below 0.2 m thins opacity; dry/grounded, deep >5 m, invalid and missing-cache fragments die immediately. Full elapsed delta governs expiration even when spray's birth-update delta is capped. The entire parent/helper group is hidden underwater.

Render budget: one extra instanced water-plane mesh, 2048 triangles; together with spray, two visible draws. Existing sampling adds its existing one draw only at request cadence. Geometry, typed arrays and shader material are constructed once and disposed idempotently. No texture or geometry is allocated per frame. Fragment-shader noise perforates soft irregular ribbons; quads face upward, not the camera. Transparent material has depth testing and no depth writes.

Approximation: transported centers follow bilinear cached FFT height, while each 1.2–5.5 m fragment is locally planar and 0.025 m above the cached surface. This can intersect a crest or hover within grid/cache interpolation error; the helper does not resolve overturning, wave lips, wave-scale flow or foam entrainment. A 6 m birth lattice with irregular alongshore jitter remains coarse. Lifetimes/advection/appearance are artistic candidate parameters, not measurements from the edited reference video. No weather, wave height or period was inferred. No visual/Human acceptance, browser/GPU shader compilation or measured frame-cost claim is made by these CPU tests.

Validation: TypeScript noEmit; six whitewater tests plus six existing spray tests pass (12 total). They cover birth negative paths, finite input, fixed capacity, analytic dt consistency, full-delta expiry, wet strand and land/deep/cache culling, surface following, underwater visibility, disposal and default-off/shared-readback integration. Core FFT, physics and external maps are untouched.

## Root GPU QA repair

Root reported that opt-in GPU compilation failed because the fragment shader declared `float patch`, a reserved GLSL word. This candidate renames it to `coverageNoise`. CPU/TypeScript checks cannot establish GPU acceptance; root must repeat actual shader compilation. Root's initial 8-second Secret capture had ready samples, no readback failure, and zero spray/foam births. Numerical birth behavior is unchanged pending readback evidence.

Diagnostics now expose maxima and counts for the latest accepted RGBA cache: `maxSampleEnergy`, `maxSampleCrest` (positive height), `sampleEnergyPositiveCount`, and `sampleWetEligibleCount` (depth 0.2–3.8 m, regardless of energy). `maxEstimatedHeightDepthRatio` is max(0,sampled instantaneous height)/depth over those wet-eligible cells; it is an instantaneous crest/depth proxy, **not** measured crest-to-trough wave height or a validated breaking criterion. `whitewaterEmitted` is cumulative successful births, distinct from current active pool size. No additional sampler/readback or changed threshold/credit behavior was introduced.
