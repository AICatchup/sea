# Crest observation v14 (operator only)

`ShoreBreaker.probeDriver(renderer, expanded=false)` preserves the legacy `available`, `origin`, and three `rows` of 30 RGBA values. Only `expanded=true` adds `profile`. It is never invoked from an update or frame callback by this component.

Expanded output has schemaVersion 1, `channels` (32 names), and three `rows`, each with `rowOffsetM` (-8, 0, +8) and 65 `samples`. Each sample is a flat 32-number array indexed by `channels`. Cross offsets are -48 through +48 metres in 1.5 metre steps. Position is `depthCrossingSource - uphillNormal * crossOffsetM + alongshoreTangent * rowOffsetM`. Thus positive cross offset goes offshore. Rows are independent parallel transects; they do not inherit the production ribbon's Hessian shear. The legacy driver still reports the real ribbon driver and gate at its sheared peak.

Units are metres for positions, depth, elevation and height; m2/s for qx/qz; 1/s for compression; other channels are dimensionless masks, ratios, confidence or proxy gates. Raw compression is the exact current `whitewaterFlowSampling` velocity-divergence stencil extracted from its source string. Ratio is the production bore ratio; the four gates expose its factors independently, while `bornProxy` is the actual `whitewaterSolvedFlow(p).z` including its validity rejection. Compression can therefore be positive while born is zero. Historical solver foam is not interpreted as birth.

`bathyValid`, `solverDomainValid`, `solverStencilValid`, and `flowBirthValid` must be checked before interpreting respective raw samples. Invalid raw values are placeholders, not measurements. Raw solver channels require bathy and solver-domain validity; compression also requires stencil validity. `flowBirthValid` additionally requires shelter >= .18 and nearest depth >= .01. A valid dry raw cell can be inspected, but cannot be a wet-front candidate. `bilinearWetSurfaceHeightM` is usable only when `bilinearWetConfidence` is positive; otherwise it is zero placeholder. `renderedSurfaceHeightM` and `incidentHeightM` intentionally preserve production fallback behavior, and do not themselves establish solver validity. Surface elevation, wet confidence and blending are instrumented from the exact production `shoreSolvedSurface` function, including its bilinear wet normalization and `shoreSurfaceBlend` helper.

For candidate analysis, exclude the first/last samples, require valid wet samples and valid neighboring samples, then compare the actual incident-height local maximum and positive births in neighboring cells. `previousIncidentHeightM` samples p+n*1.5; `nextIncidentHeightM` samples p-n*1.5. `profileLocalMaximum` is a simple >= comparison, includes ties, and is only a candidate marker; it does not certify a wet valid interior crest. `driverEndpointRejected` reports the original driver's abs(offset)>=12 rejection, repeated on each profile sample. The original 30x3 output carries the real chosen maximum's offset, refined peak, slope, curvature, birth energy, radius and full envelope. Comparing that chosen maximum to independent profile fronts avoids confusing maximum elevation with compression-front location.

Cost per expanded call: the original 30x3 RGBA32F draw/readback plus one 520x3 RGBA32F draw/readback (24,960 bytes for the latter). All temporary resources are owned and disposed after the call. Borrowed full uniform objects and textures are shared, never disposed. Render target, viewport, scissor, scissor test, autoClear, clear color and clear alpha are restored in finally, including draw/readback failure. Normal calls do not allocate the expanded GPU resources or pixel buffer.

Verification: targeted mocked renderer tests cover lane decoding, dimensions, capability absence, opt-in behavior, unchanged production shader text, borrowed resource preservation, and renderer-state restoration on expanded-draw failure. These tests and TypeScript checking are local evidence only; root owns shader compilation and live GPU time-series acquisition. No thresholds, search, appearance or production shader text were changed.
