# Connected scarp volume candidate v8

`new NiijimaScarpVolume(coast, coast.dem, pumiceMaterial)` builds one indexed closed mesh with 181 alongshore sections over an authored 260 m patch near Secret. Use an **unmodified** DEM (`scarp:false`). Add `candidate.group` to the scene and pass it in `WorldSolidBinding.sync(..., [candidate.group])` extras. Ground height queries continue to use the original coast; collision comes from the identical visible volume. Call `dispose()` after removing the group and resyncing solids. Supplied material is cloned and caller ownership is preserved.

The viewed White Mama reference shows pale pumice, irregular vertical channels, horizontal beds, shelving and loose talus above a broad dry strand. No source-image pixels are embedded. Width, bedding heights, channel depths and undercuts are authored hypotheses, not surveyed dimensions. Frontage follows source shore gradients, toes are at about 28 m inland distance, crest heights follow inland source DEM. Front, top, back and bottom are one connected closed surface, with end sections buried into terrain. Nonperiodic hash interpolation at several scales drives gullies and asymmetric relief; stepped depth changes produce shelves impossible in a single-valued terrain heightfield.

The candidate preserves the source arrays and corridor at 12 m shore distance. Perimeter burial avoids coplanar joins. Visible protrusion is intentional; existing terrain slopes may still emerge through deeply recessed channels depending on integration sampler. It is a finite prototype patch, not a replacement for the complete coast. Closed topological tests do not establish absence of all geometric self-intersections or photographic fidelity. Rendering, multiview seam checks and FPS capsule acceptance remain root integration checks. End cap fans are buried; the cap triangulation should not be exposed as a visitor-facing surface.
