# Dense coastal canopy candidate

Official CC0 Poly Haven originals: island_tree_01, island_tree_02 and island_tree_03. The provider describes these as dense wind-shaped coastal trees; botanical species is not established and these are not surveyed Tomari black pines. The original 1K photo diffuse, OpenGL normal, ARM maps and texture transforms are retained. Separate native leaf-alpha PNGs are acquired and verified against the official API; the JPEG leaf diffuse has black atlas padding and must use this alpha mask. `provenance.json` records original URLs, sizes, MD5, SHA256, original/derived metric bounds, reduction errors and derived GLB hashes.

Three distinct physical crown/trunk models are normalized to heights 4.9m, 3.8m and 3.2m, with wider wind-shaped spread. Each distance band contains real branches and individually modeled, curved photo leaves. Near foliage remains native. Middle and far disconnected leaf components receive bounded uniform expansion (2.5cm / 18cm maximum point displacement) to retain projected coverage after decimation; UVs and normals remain intact. No crown spheres, generated tree cutouts, tree billboards or emissive lighting are used. Texture alpha is tested with depth writes, shadow alpha and double-sided small leaves. Native trunks remain gray/weathered in their original photographic diffuse maps.

The field now follows high soil pockets and clustered crest cover, leaves lower steep rock exposed, and overlaps broad crowns with 3D shrubs and low leaf cover. Current integrated terrain yields 3,123 trees and 10,699 shrubs, including 4,635 low ground-cover instances. These are authored vegetation placements rather than surveyed plant positions. The 96 user placements, undo, immutable trunk-proxy API, scan-resource ownership and boat-transform ownership are retained.

Field budgets: pines 3.6M triangles, shrubs 1.35M; at most 36 active foliage draws. Standard (-42,9) yields 4,557,341 triangles and 32 draws; shore 4,762,231, cliff 4,759,993, lookout 4,789,805, distant 3,874,449. Counts are submitted geometry before view frustum culling, not GPU performance measurements. Actual root rendering still needs verification after source readiness.

At 128px CPU orthographic raster with native alpha threshold 0.38, front/side leaf coverage (near / middle / far pixels) is: variant 01: 1140 / 829 / 915 and 1001 / 705 / 782; variant 02: 952 / 720 / 801 and 803 / 571 / 558; variant 03: 385 / 403 / 273 and 381 / 372 / 296. Far retains 70-84% of near leaf coverage in these two angles. These measurements check geometric/alpha continuity, not photographic match or lighting.

The three new GLBs plus three native alpha PNGs total 23,760,426 bytes. Source headers and maps were inspected; alpha-aware front/side coverage, finite normals/positions, zero degenerate triangles, exclusive LOD partitions, 96 placements, undo, cached read-only trunk bases/radii, vessel transforms and resource disposal were checked on CPU. 55 repository tests and production/standalone build passed. GPU, physical appearance, exact lighting under root's daylight changes, and Human acceptance remain unverified.

Reacquire with `node src/assets/foliage/cc0/canopy/fetch-canopy.mjs --refresh`, followed by `node src/assets/foliage/cc0/canopy/fetch-alpha.mjs`. The second step binds native masks into the provenance receipt. Runtime uses only bundled files. Google reference photo pixels are not included.

V16 retains the source photo colour in manual linear-light, alpha-weighted colour mipmaps. Invisible atlas padding receives adjacent leaf colour; the separate native alpha map, cutoff, geometry, placement and shadow settings remain unchanged. Ordinary box filtering mixed black JPEG padding into small distant leaves (retained mean source RGB: about 0.288 at 1024px versus 0.143 at 4px for the shared leaf atlas). This corrects texture sampling, not plant density, species, surveyed botany or photographic acceptance. Original and corrected textures are owned by ModelResources. Corrected maps are created once per shared material; reprocessing an already corrected DataTexture is invalid. DEV comparison can temporarily restore original maps and verifies that three native pine materials are available. A fallback-only comparison is rejected. Actual fixed-pose comparison images and evidence limits are recorded by the root.
